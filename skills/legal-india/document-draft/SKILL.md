# Document Drafting

## When to Use

Use this when the user asks to draft a notice, plaint, written statement, reply, petition, application, agreement, appeal, memo, representation, or other Indian legal document.

Use only when the document type, facts, party perspective, grounds, and any required authorities or template hints are available or can be marked for confirmation.

## Procedure

1. Confirm the document type, domain, matter type, forum/authority, party we act for, opposing party, and intended purpose.
2. Gather confirmed facts, grounds to advance, annexures, deadlines, and template or house-style requirements.
3. Use formal Indian drafting conventions for the document type and domain.
4. Address every ground. For notices or statutory filings, include mandatory contents from the relevant domain pack or supplied template.
5. Cite only provided or retrieved authorities. Do not cite from memory.
6. Mark every unverified fact inline as `[CONFIRM: ...]` and collect open items at the end.
7. Produce a review-ready draft and a short checklist of missing facts, annexures, authorities, and filing/service steps for human review.
8. Run citation-check when authorities are used.

## Pitfalls

- Draft only. Do not serve, send, file, upload, or dispatch.
- Do not invent facts, dates, case numbers, authorities, or annexures.
- If core facts are missing, produce a structured outline or partial draft marked not ready for review.
- Do not include operational instructions that imply dispatch is authorised.

## Verification

- Every ground is addressed.
- Every cited authority comes from a provided or retrieved source.
- Every unverified fact is marked `[CONFIRM: ...]`.
- The output is a draft for advocate review, with no filing or dispatch action.
